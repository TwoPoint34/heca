Nature-based Home Solutions

Applying ecological science and geographic information systems to prepare Michigan homes for the future.

NBHS draws on deep understanding of natural processes to save our clients money and improve quality of life. We work with homes, non-profits, schools, churches, and more to conduct a "home ecology audit" that identifies low-cost, low-maintenance, nature-based solutions to pressing challenges. Whereas the common practice of a home energy audit focuses on the inside of a building, the NBHS home ecology audit extends its benefits to the building's exterior and to the yard. We combine client input, GIS analysis, and field observation to pinpoint where rainfall runoff flows on a property and identify locations for green infrastructure (esp. trees/shrubs). We also assess the feasibility of other economical and high-impact interventions, such as rooftop solar and geothermal heating/cooling. NBHS quantifies projected cost savings and environmental benefits and delivers these projections to clients in a final report. This report will also contain a host of additional information about your property that you may find useful… or at least fascinating!

I. Reduce Basement Flooding and Stormwater Infrastructure Costs

If your house experiences basement flooding, you are definitely not alone. You’d be shocked at how many contractors just skip the step of identifying where surface runoff flows/pools on your property and jump straight into expensive basement waterproofing and/or drain installation. NBHS, on the other hand, uses high-resolution LiDAR imagery plus field observation to pinpoint the source of basement flooding. In many cases, we can then design low-cost green infrastructure and/or minor earth-moving to address the root cause of the flooding. Even if your basement does not flood, green infrastructure has great potential to lower stormwater infrastructure costs, which are ultimately paid by residents.

II. Grow Food and Improve Outdoor Comfort

Growing food at home optimizes freshness, saves money, improves nutrition, fosters a sense of place, builds community, and (imho) is simply a lot of fun. NBHS will work with each client to choose trees/shrubs that balance growing food for humans with growing food for wildlife (esp. pollinators). We assess soil properties, sun angles, water flow, plant genetics, and more to ensure plant survival. The fact that trees shade out grass and don’t require replanting each year can substantially reduce long-term yard maintenance costs. Trees also improve outdoor comfort, save on utility bills via shading, and generally increase property values.

III. Improve Home Safety and Health

Humans have long struggled to manage disease-spreading pests such as mosquitoes and ticks. This situation is further complicated by the fact that Michigan’s climate is getting warmer and wetter. NBHS brings integrated, ecological solutions to this inherently ecological challenge and increases Nature's benefits while minimizing its downsides. Precision tree/shrub planting can also reduce surface icing in the winter and alleviate dangerous heat extremes in the summer. Trees also clean our air and water and offer a host of positive psychological benefits.

IV. Assess Renewable Home Energy Options

Solar power and geothermal heating/cooling are not typically categorized as “nature-based solutions.” That notwithstanding, NBHS considers it very “natural” to use sunlight for power generation and stored solar energy for ground-source heat pumps (a.k.a. geothermal). We can use GIS analysis to estimate your rooftop’s total solar power potential (kWh/year) as well as the CO2 emissions you can avoid by going solar. Although we have not yet expanded into geothermal, we believe there is potential for NBHS to analyze soil characteristics, take property measurements, and explore financing options in order to determine whether or not geothermal would be feasible for a client. Renewable home energy not only helps the planet but it can also save serious money on home utility bills.
