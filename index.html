Nature-based Home Solutions

Applying ecological science and geographic information systems to prepare Michigan homes for the future.

NBHS draws on deep understanding of natural processes to save our clients money and improve quality of life. We work with homes, non-profits, schools, churches, and more to conduct a "home ecology audit" that identifies low-cost, low-maintenance, nature-based solutions to pressing challenges. Whereas the common practice of a home energy audit focuses on the inside of a building, the NBHS home ecology audit extends its benefits to the building's exterior and to the yard. We combine client input, GIS analysis, and field observation to pinpoint where rainfall runoff flows on a property and identify locations for green infrastructure (esp. trees/shrubs). We also assess the feasibility of other economical and high-impact interventions, such as rooftop solar and geothermal heating/cooling. NBHS quantifies projected cost savings and environmental benefits and delivers these projections to clients in a final report.

I. Reduce Basement Flooding and Stormwater Infrastructure Costs

If your house experiences basement flooding, you are definitely not alone. You’d be shocked at how many contractors just skip the step of identifying where surface runoff flows/pools on your property and jump straight into expensive basement waterproofing and/or drain installation. NBHS, on the other hand, uses high-resolution LiDAR imagery plus field observation to pinpoint the source of basement flooding. In many cases, we can then design low-cost green infrastructure and/or minor earth-moving to address the root cause of the flooding. Even if your basement does not flood, green infrastructure has great potential to lower stormwater infrastructure costs, which are ultimately paid by residents.

II. Grow Food and Improve Outdoor Comfort

Cost Savings: • Establish trees and shrubs that produce food year after year • Reduce area of yard that requires mowing • Improve outdoor comfort and save on utility bills through shading from trees • Capture and make use of stormwater and thereby save on water bills Growing food at home optimizes freshness, saves money, improves nutrition, fosters a sense of place, builds community, and is simply a lot of fun. If clients choose edible perennial plants as part of their green infrastructure, NBHS assesses soil properties, sun angles, water flow, plant genetics, and more to ensure plant survival and drastically reduce long-term maintenance needs.

III. Improve Home Safety and Health

Humans have long struggled to manage disease-spreading pests such as mosquitoes and ticks. This situation is further complicated by a warmer and wetter climate. NBHS brings integrated, ecological solutions to this inherently ecological challenge and increases Nature's benefits while minimizing its downsides.

IV. Support Native Biodiversity

Join a vibrant and growing community of people working ultra-locally to protect our imperiled natural wealth. NBHS supports native wildlife through: planting native species, controlling invasives, and capturing carbon in biomass. In addition to reducing stormwater runoff, the simple act of mowing less often within runoff channels can dramatically increase lightning bugs and critical pollinators such as bees and butterflies.
